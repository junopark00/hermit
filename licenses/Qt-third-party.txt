Third-party components of Qt 6.11.3
====================================

Qt 6.11.3 (Qt Base, Qt Declarative, Qt SVG) is shipped with Hermit as DLLs and plugins. This file
lists the third-party components recorded in the SPDX software bills of materials of the
Qt 6.11.3 MSVC 2022 x64 build (sbom/qtbase-6.11.3.spdx.json, qtdeclarative-6.11.3.spdx.json,
qtsvg-6.11.3.spdx.json in the Qt installation). Some of them are used only on other platforms,
in tests or while building Qt.

Licenses are given as SPDX identifiers; their full texts are at https://spdx.org/licenses/.
Licenses specific to Qt ("LicenseRef-...") are reproduced at the end of this file. Qt's own
list of third-party code and licenses: https://doc.qt.io/qt-6/licenses-used-in-qt.html

This software is based in part on the work of the Independent JPEG Group (libjpeg-turbo, used
by the qjpeg image format plugin).
